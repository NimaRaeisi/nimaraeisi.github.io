## Research and Project Experience

### Building a Hydro-Thermal Model in Discontinuous Permafrost, Giant Mine Remediation Project
*PhD research, University of Calgary* — May 2026 – Present

- Building a coupled hydro-thermal model of the Baker Creek watershed to study how thawing permafrost changes the exchange between groundwater and the creek.
- Characterizing site conditions with ERT surveys, geotechnical testing and a conceptual site model.
- Comparing alternative Baker Creek diversion designs for their effect on thaw and groundwater flow.
- Running long-term climate scenarios to predict how the watershed responds as permafrost degrades.

### Permafrost and Water Storage Change in the Mackenzie River Basin
*Independent remote sensing project, University of Calgary* — 2026 – Present

- Built a Python workflow that removes snow and soil moisture from GRACE/GRACE-FO satellite gravity data (2002–2026), producing 36 water-storage series for six subbasins in continuous, discontinuous and permafrost-free zones.
- Tested trends with pre-whitened Mann–Kendall and Sen's slope, then checked them against HYDAT baseflow, precipitation and lake-level records.
- Found that storage fell in every subbasin: least in continuous permafrost (−0.33 cm/yr) and most in the permafrost-free zone (−0.80 cm/yr).
